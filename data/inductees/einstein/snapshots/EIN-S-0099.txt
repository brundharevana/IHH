Description
Jack Burnett's photographs document the interior of Albert Einstein's former residence at 112 Mercer Street in Princeton, New Jersey. The photographs were taken in 1969 at the behest of Otto Nathan, the co-trustee with Helen Dukas of Einstein's literary estate.
