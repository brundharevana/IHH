Summary

left to right: Dr. Albert Abraham Michelson (1852-1931), Professor of Physics, University of Chicago; Dr. Albert Einstein (1879-1955), Berlin Academy of Sciences; and Dr. Robert Andrews Millikan (1868-1953), director, Norman Bridge Laboratory of Physics, California Institute of Technology. Michelson had received the Nobel Prize for Physics in 1907; Einstein, in 1921; and Millikan, in 1923. One of a series of photographs taken at The Athenaeum, California Institute of Technology, February 1931.
