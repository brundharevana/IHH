title: Letter from Albert Einstein and the Emergency Committee of Atomic Scientists
scopeAndContentNote: In this letter, Albert Einstein writes as Chairman of the Emergency Committee of Atomic Scientists discussing the issue of international atomic energy control. In the letter Albert Einstein warns his audience about international ingenuity, the need for international cooperation, and expresses his candor regarding the destruction of the human race. Additionally, Einstein pushes for an effort in public education about atomic energy and includes the article "Atomic War or Peace," authored by Albert Einstein. "Atomic War or Peace" details Einstein's thoughts regarding international politics, especially between the United States and Russia. Einstein discusses his opinion on the need of a supranational agreement for the regulation and use of nuclear weaponry. Einstein also reflects on ways to form this organization. Einstein elaborates on world economic interests within a nuclear world and its effects on political policy on nations.
