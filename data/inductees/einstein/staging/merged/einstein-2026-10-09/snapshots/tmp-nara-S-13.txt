title: Letter from Albert Einstein, Chairman of the Emergency Committee of Atomic Scientists
scopeAndContentNote: In this letter, Albert Einstein wrote on behalf of the Emergency Committee of Atomic Scientists regarding questions, topics, and warnings concerning the use of nuclear or atomic resources as a means for diplomatic or military solutions. Einstein also wrote and included in the correspondence a statement from the Emergency Committee of Atomic Scientists that details the events that the committee deemed pitfalls within the international community regarding the development, use, and defense for and against atomic war.
