Letter from John Muir to Mary [Muir Hand], 1903 Apr 29.

Creator

John Muir

Creator

John Muir

Recipient

Mary [Muir Hand]

Transcription

Martinez, Apr 19, 1903.

Dear Mary, If you have father's naturalization papers will you please forward them to me as quickly as possible. I need them in applying for a passport.
We are all about as usual. Ever affectionately

your brother John.

Martinez [Calif.]
