## Method

Run ids: muir-tour-2026-10-15 (merge of 39 facts, 8 sources, 1 media, 14 search rows),
muir-tour-repair-2026-10-15 (7 facts, 1 row), muir-tour-repair2-2026-10-15 (4 facts, 1 row). Every
search and fetch is logged in searches.jsonl as MUI-Q-0186 to MUI-Q-0201 with what it produced.

1. Read the skill, SPEC, PATTERNS and LESSONS; listed the 91 facts and 88 sources on file and their
   status (before: verified 21, single-source 52, disputed 18; media verified 31, pending 10,
   disputed 2).
2. Re-read snapshots already on file (NPS timeline MUI-S-0017, NPS Yosemite MUI-S-0020, Sierra Club
   chronology MUI-S-0024, HABS MUI-S-0047, HALS MUI-S-0048, Smithsonian Archives MUI-S-0080,
   University of the Pacific chronology MUI-S-0087) for stop-level dates and places, preferring
   claims two registered holders state in their own words.
3. WebSearch (8 queries) for the Martinez study, Fountain Lake Farm NHL, Yosemite cabin and sawmill,
   the Dunbar birthplace, the Pacific 1879 journal, the George W. Elder, North Hall and Muir's cabin
   drawing. Wikipedia appeared only as a discovery hit and is never cited.
4. Fetched new sources with tools/fetch_snapshot.py (http) where it worked: four NPS pages, two
   Pacific pages. The NHL nomination is a scanned PDF: downloaded with curl, text layer extracted
   with pdftotext, saved as a manual snapshot (MUI-S-0089). NPGallery timed out for the fetch tool,
   so the Lorne Hotel record was saved from curl HTML as a manual snapshot (MUI-S-0095).
   Failures logged: LoC full text of "The Story of My Boyhood and Youth" (no OCR text in the JSON),
   Martinez NHL short form (OCR unreadable), HFR PDF (HTML returned), Pacific drawing jmd/1186 (403),
   SIA ship photo (403), NPGallery NRIS pages and LoC searches for coordinates (none).
5. Wrote each claim in its source's own words and checked it before merging with the gate functions
   (gates.unsupported_tokens and gates.whole_claim_gaps per source) and normalize.contains_normalized
   for each quote; six claims were reworded (dates written as "In 1838, on April 21"; "moves" for
   "moved") so the quote covers them.
6. merge_staging.py, compute_status.py, validate.py (exit 0) after each merge.
7. Adversary: tools/adversary.py --engine codex --model gpt-6.1-sol (codex-cli 0.162.1):
   run adv-20261015T003839-09cf on 50 pending items (34 supported, 13 refuted, 3 unclear); a
   re-judgement of the 18 disputed older facts whose verdicts predated per-source live checks (17
   supported, 1 unclear); 7 repair facts (7 supported); 6 facts again (6 supported); 4 restaged
   facts (4 supported).
8. Repairs after the adversary: MUI-F-0093 and MUI-F-0094 withdrawn (the NPS title separates the
   boyhood home from the birth building); MUI-F-0117 and MUI-F-0121 split (not atomic); MUI-F-0127
   restaged as MUI-F-0135 (event_date was the earthquake year); MUI-F-0130 restaged as MUI-F-0136 and
   MUI-F-0137; MUI-F-0112, 0114, 0115, 0116 restaged as MUI-F-0138 to 0141 without the Sierra Club
   vault chronology, which the adversary could not load live in two runs (G10).
9. Side effect: the 10 older media items that had no verdict (Commons-sourced or LoC records without
   a rights statement) were judged in the same run and are now disputed (no holder rights statement).
