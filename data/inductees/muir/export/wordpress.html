DRAFT — NOT REVIEWED John Muir was a writer, a preservationist and a conservationist.[1][2][3][4][5][6][7] John Muir co-founded the Sierra Club in 1892.[8][9][10][11]

DRAFT — NOT REVIEWED Hetch Hetchy Valley

DRAFT — NOT REVIEWED John Muir and other leading preservationists signed a petition that the Society for the Preservation of National Parks submitted to Congress.[12] The petition urged Congress to defeat the Raker Bill, which called for a dam to be built across the Hetch Hetchy Valley.[12]
