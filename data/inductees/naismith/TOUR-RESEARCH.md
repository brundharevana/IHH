## Method

1. Read the skill, SPEC, PATTERNS, LESSONS and the 93 facts, 69 sources, 32 media already on file.
   Mapped each stop to what was already verified and to which holders could corroborate it.
2. Searched (all logged, NAI-Q-0233..0270): LoC JSON search (5 queries), Chronicling America
   (3 date-bounded queries, OCR read through LoC Text Services), LoC Today in History (5 pages),
   WebSearch on ku.edu, springfield.edu, hoophall.com, si.edu and the Spencer blog (9 queries),
   the KU Libraries Omeka exhibit (5 timeline pages, 30 item pages), Digital Commonwealth JSON
   (5 searches, 7 records), Springfield College CONTENTdm API and IIIF (6 records, for sizes and
   coordinates only), NRCan CGNDB, OpenStreetMap Nominatim.
3. Fetched snapshots with `tools/fetch_snapshot.py` (http for KU pages, api for LoC). The two
   CONTENTdm pages are JavaScript; their text was copied with the in-app browser and saved with
   `--method browser`. The LoC book page (Robinson Gymnasium caption) was refused by
   fetch_snapshot (its fulltext_file and fulltext_service disagree), and loc.gov answered the browser
   with a bot check, which was not bypassed; the item record was saved instead.
4. Wrote each claim in its sources' own words and checked locally, per source, with
   `gates.whole_claim_gaps` before staging, so G5 counted only sources that covered the whole claim.
   Sources from one holder were not paired for corroboration.
5. `merge_staging.py` (28 facts, 16 sources, 12 media, 37 searches; then 3 facts, 1 search),
   `compute_status.py`, Codex adversary on 40 items: 30 supported, 5 refuted, 5 unclear. A first
   adversary attempt failed (HTTP 400, model not supported) because `/opt/homebrew/bin/codex` 0.156.0
   shadows the nvm codex 0.162.1 on PATH; it was re-run with the nvm binary first on PATH.
6. Repairs: NAI-F-0106 withdrawn (refuted: the dateline dates the report, not the event);
   NAI-F-0107 and NAI-F-0113 withdrawn as not atomic and replaced by NAI-F-0122, -0123, -0124;
   five place-photo media withdrawn on the lead's instruction. Three facts staged as `conflicting`
   with each source's version in notes. `validate.py naismith` exit 0.
