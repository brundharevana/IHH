CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
FOR THE USE OF
PHYSICAL DEPARTMENT OF THE YOUNG MEN'S CHRISTIAN ASSOCIATION TRAINING SCHOOL
SPRINGFIELD, MASS.

[Note: In office of Physical Director of the Young Men's Christian Association in sending in your application, kindly answer the following questions as fully as possible.]

1. Date: March 27th, 1891
2. Full name: James Naismith
3. Address: Almonte, Ont.
4. When born: Almonte, Ont.
5. Date of birth: Nov 6, 1861
6. Height: 5 ft 10 3/4 in.
7. Weight: 168 lbs.
8. Are you a college graduate? Yes If so, of what college and when were you graduated? McGill 1887
9. Are you married? No If so, how many children have you? If so, of what college and when were you graduated?
10. If you have had educational advantages not here implied?
11. What is the name and address of the pastor of the church with which you are connected? Rev. Jno. A. McKellar, Almonte, Ont.
12. How long have you been a Christian? 10 years
13. Have you had experience in teaching Sunday School, Bible Class, or other religious work? Sunday School teacher in Almonte and Montreal
14. What is your height? 5 ft 10 3/4 in. Weight with clothes on 168 lbs. Weight without clothes 165 lbs.
15. Have you had experience in the work of a gymnasium and athletics, such as baseball, football, cricket, track athletics, or tennis? I have played football for 8 years, baseball for 5 years, lacrosse for 5 years, and have done a little in track athletics. I have had experience in gymnasium work for 3 years.
16. What experience have you had in leading devotional meetings? Where and for how long? Led prayer meetings in McGill College for 3 years.
17. What experience have you had in dealing with young men? Where and for how long? Led prayer meetings in McGill College for 3 years.
18. What success have you had in winning young men to Christ?
19. What are your present intentions when you leave the school? To become a physical director in a Y.M.C.A.
20. What is the work of a Y.M.C.A. Physical Director? To look after the gymnasium, to teach classes, to look after the athletics, to look after the health of the members, to teach them how to take care of themselves, and to help them in their spiritual life.
21. Have you a recent photograph of yourself? Yes

In what do you need help? Football

Return to Almonte, Ont., care of J.A. McKellar, Springfield, Mass.
In order to defray the expense of investigations, each applicant enters with this blank, TWENTY-FIVE CENTS in stamps.
