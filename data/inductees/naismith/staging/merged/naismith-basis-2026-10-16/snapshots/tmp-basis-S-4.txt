Title
Annie Naismith outside the birthplace of Dr. James Naismith

Description
A photograph of Annie Naismith, the sister of Dr. James Naismith, outside the old family house and birthplace of James Naismith. The house was located in Almonte, Canada, now part of town of Mississippi Mills. The picture shows her in a long, heavy coat and hat, standing in the snow outside the house. The photograph shows the porch and side of the house. This photograph was part of a group of 6 photographs donated by Jim Naismith, the grandson of Dr. Naismith, and originally scanned and sent to him by Marilyn Snedden who lives near Bennies Corners near Almonte, Ontario.
