Title
U.S. Postage Stamp Commemorating Dr. James Naismith, 1961

Description
A photographic print of a U.S. Postage stamp commemorating Dr. James Naismith as the creator of the game of basketball.  The title of this particular stamp is "Birth Centenary of Dr. James A. Naismith." It was worth four cents. The picture on the stamp is of someone laying a basketball up into the hoop.  Down the left side of the stamp reads, "Naismith 1861-1961."
