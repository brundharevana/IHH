HE INVENTED IT That the lord mayor is not suffering suffer-ing from an overdose of dignity was evidenced when he arrived in this country and was questioned by reporters report-ers as to how one in his position should be addressed. He rather astonished as-tonished news hawks by telling them : "Just call me Alfie !" When the National Basketball Coaches' association held its annual convention in Chicago, the most distinguished dis-tinguished man present was Dr. James Naismith of the University of Kansas, for it was he who invented the game.
