Gentlemen:

I am writing this letter as the attorney for Miss Hedy Lamarr of 2702 Benedict Canyon, Beverly Hills, California who by marriage became Mrs. Hedwig Loder and who is a British subject.

(Handwritten ‘British’ in margin.)

The purpose for this letter is to ask for your protection of Mrs. Loder’s property interests in the oilfields of Zistersdorf in Austria.

(Handwritten ‘Russian Zone’ in margin.)

Mrs. Hedwig Loder’s father, the late Emil Kiesler of Vienna, was the owner of a 30% share and a three gross percentum interest in all goods mined or to be mined in Zistersdorf, a place in the north-eastern most corner of the Province of Lower Austria, a few miles northeasterly of Vienna. The late Emil Kiesler had set up a number of companies and more specifically the Erdoel- Bohr- und Verner-tungsgesellschaft m.b.H., Gewerkschaft , , Pionier , , “Soha” Rohoelgesellschaft m.b.H. and Steinberg – Naphta – Gesellschaft m.b.H., all Austrian companies, for the purpose of experimental drilling so as to find out whether the lands on which the companies had acquired drilling and mining rights contained minerals and oil in quantities sufficient to warrant the establishment of financially stronger corporations that, equipped with larger funds, would take over with the purpose of mining and drilling on a larger scale. It was agreed that in case the experimental drilling would show good results, the shareholders of the experimental companies, as named above, will be rewarded by the industrial enterprises to be set up and that for the assignment of their drilling and mining rights, as well as for their work, risks, and expenses , they will receive a certain percentage of shares in the new industrial enterprise.

Mrs. Hedwig Loder’s father died in 1935 and his interests in the oil fields, among them in Zistersdorf, were inherited by Mrs. Hedwig Loder, his only child, together with Mrs. Gertrude Kiesler, her mother.

Mrs.Hedwig Loder, as a resident of the United States, and a British
subject, was unable to keep up correspondence with, and obtain in-
formation from the people in charge of the Zistersdorf oil fields
since December 7,1941, since Zistersdorf was enemy occupied territo-
ry since that day.It is, however, known from newspaper publications
that meanwhile a huge industry was built up in Zistersdorf, that the
Zistersdorf field was developed by the enemy to a point where its
output was one fourth of that of\the Rumanian oil fields and that the
enemy appropriated 51% of the Zistersdorf stock from its former
Austrian and foreign owners.

Furthermore, according to a report dated October 6th and published
in the New York Times on October 8th by John MacCormac, the Red Ar-
my has taken forcible possession of the Zistersdorf field claiming
this most important asset, persuant to the Potdam agreement ,as for-
merly German held stock.

It also appears from Mr.MacCormac's report that there was some dis-
cussion and that one of the arguments was that the Zistersdorf oil
fields are formerly Austrian Property. It also appears that nowhere
in the discussion the fact that part of Zistersdorf is property of
a British subject, resident of the United States, was taken into
consideration.

It is Mrs.Hedwig Loder's intention to take, in time, all possible
steps in the direction of the protection of her property rights in
Zistersdorf and therefore, Mrs. Hedwig Loder hereby invokes your
protection since, as an American resident, for the time being, she
is unable to take any other steps directly in Austria

Finally I wish to add that Mrs.Loder's interests, as quoted above,
are evidenced by the Probate File # 1A 2ol-35 of the District Court
of Doebling - Vienna,Austria.

Your report on steps taken for the protection of Mrs.Hedwig Loder's
interests will be highly appreciated.

Sir,
Your letter of 7 Dec 45 addressed to U.S.
Reparations, Deliveries and Restitution Division has been
forwarded to us for attention.

The information which you give regarding
Mrs. Hedwig Loder's interest in the Zistersdorf oilfields
is noted with interest.

According to our records the Erdoel-Bohr- und
Verwertungsgesellschaft m.b.H and Gewerschaft "Pionier"
still exist, but the Steinberg Naphta concera is now an
Aktiengesellschaft. The "Scha" Rohoelgesellschaft is
not known to us.

Our information is that on 13 Mar 38 the shares
in Steinberg Naphta A.G were held as to S.1,461,700 (= 97.45%)
by a British company called City & General Enterprises Ltd.,
as to 33,300 (= 2.22%) by the representatives of your clients
father, and as to the remaining 5,000 (= .33%) by Count Antoine
Seilern. At the same date the above-named British company
also held about 96% of the capital of Gewerkschaft "Pionier"
and 100% of that of the Erdoel-Bohr concern.

On 21 Oct 38 an agreement known as "the Lucerne
Agreement" was entered into between the City company, Internationale
Tiefbohr Kommanditgesellschaft (ITAG), Steinberg Naphta, the
Pinoier and the Erdoel-Bohr concerns, whereby on certain terms
and subject to certain conditions the City company was to
transfer to Steinberg Naphta shares of the latter company to
the nominal value of RM 600,000 (and all its hares in the
Pionier and Erdoel-Bohr concerns). Steinberg Naphta were
then to transfer [unreadable handwritten correction], to ITAG, with the result that Steinberg Naphta
would then have been owned as to 60% by ITAG and as to 40% by
the City company.

In 1940 certain alterations in Steinberg Naphta's
capital structure were made which resulted in 47% of its
capital being held by ITAG, 47% by I.G. Farben industrie, and
6% by the City company. It would seem that the Kiesler holding
must have been acquired in the meantime; by what method has not
yet been ascertained. In 1944 ITAG sold its holding to the
Deutsche Arbeitsfront. Representatives of the City company have
expressed the intention to attack the validity of the Lucerne
agreement, and of the transactions effected under it, so soon as
the appropriate tribunal has been set up to deal with such claims.
Their interests are being watched by Dr. Rudolf Auer, Lawyer, of
32 Wipplingerstrasse, Vienna I, who is in close contact with this
HQ and with whom it is strongly recommended you should get in
touch on this aspect of the matter.

With regard to the reports of Soviet interference
mentioned by you, it is true that they have asserted their
right under the Potsdam agreement to take over the German
owned portions of the Austrian Oil Industry, in which they
