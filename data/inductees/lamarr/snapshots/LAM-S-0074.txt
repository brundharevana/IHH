The City Executive Committee has voted unanimously to sub
mit its membership to the electorate on May 13. Its previous de
cision to maintain itself in office cs a defdeto bddy was ill-advised,
undemocratic and not in line wi h the election processes.

than present boundaries it is doubtful that citizens will quibble.
More important was the issue that each four years city office
holders make themselves available o nthe printed ballot for ap

There will be suspicion that an election using the new ward

change the present method of electing executive committee mem
bers.
The main thing is that court action to compel an election has
been halted. We're glad the choice of on open election was made.
Compulsion never is agreeable.
It would have been a dangerous precedent for the election
not to have been held. There are forces all over the world that
want to do away with elections. Where they have succeeded the
people have been shackled and the menacing spectre of bondage
has hovered like an evil and fearful thing. Let this never happen
in Atlanta.
The odious device of the hand ballot may boomerang. This
is a caprice of fate which apparently the City Executive Committee
chose to ignore.
By stalling tactics, the Committee made this move necessary.
Whether regular voters will go for the hand ballot is not known
at this time but a spirited campaign to educate our voters never
theless will be undertaken.

had to happen because it is an obvious blight on our goal for a
