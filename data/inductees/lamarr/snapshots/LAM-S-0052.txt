By United Press
NANKING, Sept. 20—The United States embassy
staff moved today to the gunboat Luzon, anchored in
the river, as a result of the Japanese warning that
Nanking would be destroyed by aerial bombs.
The Luzon will remain in mid-stream until tomorrow
morning, when it probably will go up river for safety.
The bombing was to begin at Tuesday noon, Shanghai
time, or Monday midnight, Washington time.
