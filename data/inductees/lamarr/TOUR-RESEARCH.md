## Method

1. Read the skill, SPEC, PATTERNS and LESSONS, and the existing lamarr records (89 facts, 48 sources,
   21 media).
2. Re-judged the 11 facts disputed under the legacy per-fact G10 rule with Codex. `--model gpt-6.1-sol`
   (as LESSONS 2026-10-14 says) fails on codex-cli 0.156.0 with HTTP 400, "not supported with a
   ChatGPT account". `gpt-6-sol` (listed in `~/.codex/models_cache.json`) works. Result: 9 supported,
   1 refuted (LAM-F-0082, Smithsonian omits "Actor"). LAM-F-0020/-0064/-0065 moved to verified and
   LAM-F-0068 to single-source; the rest are blocked by Britannica.
3. Chronicling America (loc.gov JSON API, Text Services OCR) for the 1937-1938 crossing and arrival:
   about 10 queries. Found the October 4, 1937 wire caption (New York) and the Sept. 20, 1937 divorce
   caption. Nothing names the ship.
4. NARA catalog proxy API:
   - Naturalization file 335708097: page images read in the Browser pane.
   - Patent case file 167820368: 39 volunteer page transcriptions from the contributions endpoint.
     Petition page checked against its image.
5. USPTO: Patent Public Search API, USOCR record US-2292387-A (filing 1941-06-10, publication
   1942-08-11, full text). The official PDF is image only.
6. Wien Geschichte Wiki and LBI fetched for leads. Neither was staged: German text cannot back
   English claims under G2, and the LBI page is a podcast narrative.
7. Searched LoC and UCLA for place photos. Got rights and resolution from the holders' JSON and IIIF
   manifests. Coordinates are from the Wikidata P625 API (the coord_source `tools/fetch_coords.py`
   reads).
8. Staging: 4 sources, 10 facts, 1 media item, 21 searches. Then `merge_staging.py`, then
   `adversary.py --engine codex --model gpt-6-sol` (9 supported, 2 refuted).
   - LAM-F-0098 was reworded as LAM-F-0100, which Codex supported (single-source, secondary).
   - Then ran `compute_status.py` and `validate.py lamarr` (exit 0).
9. No aggregate export was run, and the registry was not edited.
