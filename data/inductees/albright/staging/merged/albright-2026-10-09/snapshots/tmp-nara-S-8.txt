title: Photographs Relating to Secretary Madeleine K. Albright
scopeAndContentNote: This series consists of photographs relating to the official activities of U.S. Secretary of State Madeleine K. Albright. Photos in this series include views of Albright in various capacities as Secretary of State including, but not limited to, the travels of the Secretary around the world; meetings with foreign heads of state as well as foreign ministers; the United Nations General Assembly; Secretary Albright with staff; and carrying out other official duties. The images in this series were taken by the Secretary's Official Photographer, Michael Gross.
