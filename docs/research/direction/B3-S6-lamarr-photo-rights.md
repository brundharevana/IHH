# B3-S6: Hedy Lamarr source photograph, US public-domain verification

Run 2026-10-08. Goal: a verifiably public-domain (US) photograph of Hedy Lamarr usable as the source for a painted portrait on the non-profit Immigrant Hall of Honor site. Research only; no commits, nothing installed.

Evidence tags: [V] read on the holder's page, API or scan; [P] my own measurement or search on 2026-10-08; [S] secondary source; [J] my judgement; [U] not verified.

The remaining doubt is the underlying photograph (section 3.1), which a Copyright Office search report would close.

## 2. Candidates

### 2.1 Rank 1: Screenland, November 1940 (v. 42 no. 1), cover portrait of Hedy Lamarr by Laszlo Willinger

- URL: https://archive.org/details/screenland42unse (bound volume, Nov 1940 to Apr 1941). Cover is leaf n6: https://archive.org/download/screenland42unse/page/n6_w1400.jpg (returned 2771 x 3950, 1.68 MB) [P].
- Holder of the scan: Library of Congress, Motion Picture, Broadcasting and Recorded Sound Division (contributor "Library of Congress, MBRS, Moving Image Section"; same as sponsor).
- Rights text, verbatim [V]:
  - Internet Archive metadata field `possible-copyright-status`: "Library of Congress has determined that this item is not in copyright"
  - Masthead (OCR): "Copyright 1940 by Screenland Magazine, Inc."
  - Contents page (OCR): "Cover Portrait of Hedy Lamarr by Lazlo Willinger"
  - Stamp on the cover: "NOV -5 1940 ... Library of Congress ... Periodical Division" (left edge, clear of the face)
  - Copyright Office, Catalog of Copyright Entries, New Series, Part 2 Periodicals 1940 (Vol 35), leaf n489 of https://archive.org/details/catalogofcopyrig352lib (read from the page image): "Screenland. (C) Screenland magazine, inc. ... v. 42, 1940, nos. 1, Nov. (C) Oct. 4; B 475156. 2, Dec. (C) Nov. 1; B 477153."
  - Renewal record: Online Books Page (Penn), Screenland copyright information, updated 3 Oct 2022: "First renewed issue: no issue renewals found in CCE or CPRS. First renewed contribution in: no contribution renewals found in CCE or CPRS." https://onlinebooks.library.upenn.edu/webbin/cinfo/screenland. Its serial listing adds: "No issue or contribution copyright renewals were found for this serial."
- My own cross-checks [P]: full-text search of the CCE Periodicals volumes for 1967, 1968, 1969, 1970 and 1971 (the years in which the Nov 1940 and Oct 1942 issues would have been renewed, and the lag years) shows "Screenland Plus TV-Land" only as current registrations and index lines, never marked (R), while the 1971 index marks Photoplay Movie Mirror and Silver Screen (R). Search of CCE Works of Art 1938-1942 (photograph registrations) and 1968-1971 (renewals) for "Lamarr", "Hedy" and "Willinger" returned nothing relevant (OCR search, so a weak negative).
- Confidence: medium (upper end). The issue-level facts are strong: registered, noticed, not renewed in the 28th year (1968), so the issue copyright lapsed at the end of 1968. A Library of Congress deposit stamp and a CCE line prove the registration; Penn and my searches show no renewal.
- Remaining doubt:
  1. The photograph beneath the cover. If MGM or Willinger separately published it earlier with its own notice and renewed it in 1968, the cover would not clear it. I found no such registration or renewal, but OCR search of the CCE is not a certified search. The usual rule that a magazine's notice protects contributions, and so lapses with it, is case law (Goodis v. United Artists, 2d Cir. 1970, from memory [U]), not statute.
  2. Penn states its data "may still have inadvertent errors and omissions"; it is a compilation, not an official record.
  3. Print defects: the masthead overlaps the crown of the hair, halftone dot structure, mild colour cast. Hair crown would need to be reconstructed.
  4. Right of publicity and likeness for a museum portrait is a separate question not covered here.
- Closing step: order a Copyright Office Records Research search for (a) renewal of Screenland v. 42 no. 1 (reg. B 475156) and (b) any photograph registration or renewal naming Lamarr or Laszlo Willinger, 1939-1941 and 1967-1969.

### 2.2 Rank 2: frame from Algiers (1938)

- Film: Algiers, Walter Wanger Productions, released through United Artists, directed by John Cromwell; Lamarr's American debut.
- Registration, Copyright Office, Catalog of Copyright Entries, Cumulative Series, Motion Pictures 1912-1939 (https://archive.org/details/Motionpict19121939librrich0010), entry read from OCR [V]: "ALGIERS. Released through United Artists. 1938, 10 reels, sd. From the novel by Detective Ashelbe [pseud. of Henri La Barthe]. Credits: Director, John Cromwell; screenplay, John Howard Lawson; additional dialogue, James M. Cain; ... (C) Walter Wanger Productions, Inc.; 8Aug38; LP8193." The 1938 annual volume lists it as "(M. P.) 5806", Walter Wanger productions, inc.
- Renewal window: first term ran from 1938; renewal was due in 1965-1966 [J]. I searched the three CCE Motion Pictures and Filmstrips volumes that cover it:
  - Vol 19 (1965), Vol 20 (1966), Vol 21 (1967) (https://archive.org/details/catalogofc19653191213libr, .../catalogofc19663201213libr, .../catalogofc19673211213libr).
  - No hit for "Algiers", "LP8193" or "8Aug38" in any of the three, by grep of the full text and by Internet Archive search-inside [P].
  - In the renewal listings the alphabetical gap is visible: 1966 runs "ALFALFA'S AUNT ... ALIBI ... ALMOST A GENTLEMAN" (no Algiers); 1965 runs "ALCATRAZ ISLAND ... ALPINE CABARET"; 1967 runs "ADVENTURES OF TOM THUMB, JR. ... ALIAS THE DEACON" [P].
  - Control: the same volumes do list renewals of other 1938-39 Wanger films ("BLOCKADE ... (C) 7Jun38; L8068 ... 28May65; R362176" in 1965; "WINTER CARNIVAL ... 10Aug66; R391766" in 1966; STAGECOACH in 1967), so a Wanger renewal would have been caught [V].
- Secondary source [S]: Wikipedia, "Algiers (1938 film)": "In 1966, the film entered the public domain in the United States because the claimants did not renew its copyright registration in the 28th year after publication." It cites David Pierce, "Forgotten Faces: Why Some of Our Cinema Heritage Is Part of the Public Domain", Film History 19(2), 2007, pp. 125-43. I did not read Pierce.
- Frame sources (Internet Archive; the IA licence field is uploader-set, not authoritative):
  - https://archive.org/details/Algiers_868: h.264 620 x 480, 618 MB; AVI 496 x 384, 734 MB; 99 min.
  - https://archive.org/details/Algiers_ (most downloaded, 118,054): h.264 600 x 480, 620 MB.
  - https://archive.org/details/algiers-pnm: 496 x 384 mp4, 600 MB; the uploader states the other copies are lossy derivatives of this file.
  - Colourised copies (algiers-1938-colorized, algiers-1983-colorized-movie-576p-sd) add new matter and are not PD sources.
  - I found no HD scan. I did not download any video (hundreds of MB; over the 2 MB cap), so face size in a close-up (about 150 to 200 px tall [J]) is an estimate.
- Confidence: high on the film's status.
- Remaining doubt:
  1. Resolution is the real limit; a 150-px face is thin for a painted portrait.
  2. The film is a remake of Pepe le Moko and is credited as "from the novel by Detective Ashelbe [Henri La Barthe]" (French, 1931). If the French novel's US copyright was restored under the URAA, that restored right would be a separate issue for the story, not for a still of an actress's face; I did not check restoration [U]. Practical risk low.
  3. CCE evidence is my reading of OCR; the Copyright Office can issue a certified search.

### 2.4 Rank 4: UCLA Library, Los Angeles Times Photographic Archive (CC BY 4.0, not PD)

- Items (via Wikimedia Commons, which mirrors UCLA): "Hedy Lamarr, 1939" (19 Jan 1939, 4203 x 5392; source https://digital.library.ucla.edu/catalog/ark:/21198/zz002d9663) and "Hedy Lamarr at the Tales of Manhattan premiere" (5 Aug 1942, 4059 x 5181; source .../zz0002v4v7). I viewed small thumbnails only. The 1939 file is a strong studio-style portrait; the 1942 file is a full-length press shot.
- Holder: UCLA Library Special Collections. UCLA's site sits behind a bot-challenge page, so I did not read its rights field and did not try to get past it [P].
- Rights text [V]:
  - Commons licence template: "Copyright for the photographs in the archive was deeded to UCLA. The bulk of images digitized from the archive have been published by UCLA under a CC BY 4.0 license."
  - UCLA finding aid (OAC, https://www.oac.cdlib.org/findaid/ark:/13030/kt7489n8x1/admin/): "Copyright to portions of this collection has been assigned to the UCLA Library Special Collections." Credit line: "The Regents of the University of California on behalf of the UCLA Library Special Collections."
- Confidence: medium that the licence is valid for the 1942 press photo; low-medium for the 1939 portrait.
- Remaining doubt: this is a licence, not public domain, and attribution is mandatory; "portions" of the archive were assigned, and the 1939 image looks like a copy negative of a studio still (marks at the corner), so UCLA may not own the underlying photograph. Not recommended unless the PD requirement is relaxed.

### 2.5 Rank 5: NARA, Records of the Office of War Information (RG 208), "Lamarr, Hedy"

- URL: https://catalog.archives.gov/id/178991813 (file unit, local identifier 208-WP-16V, Box 16 Folder V, National Archives at College Park Still Pictures). Not digitized [V].
- Rights text [V]: use restriction status "Restricted - Possibly"; note "Copyright restrictions may apply to the use of any items credited to a private photographer or company. Such items comprise more than half the series." The parent series (NAID 535965, "Photographs of Wartime Personalities") names Lamarr and explains the credit codes: "The two or three-letter alphabetical code following the negative number included on each mount refers to photographic source (EXAMPLE- PME, for Acme Newspictures, Inc.; FA, for U.S. Army)."
- Meaning: a print credited to the US Army or OWI itself would be a federal work and PD; a print credited to Acme or a studio would not.
- Confidence: unrated. Next step: email stillpix@nara.gov and ask for the credit codes and a scan of the folder's prints.

### 3.4 Route 4: NARA naturalization and patent files

- Naturalization: NAID 335708097, "Petition for Naturalization for Hedwig Maria Stauffer", National Archives at Riverside, box 325, 1939-1953, use restriction "Unrestricted", access "Unrestricted"; scope note: "certificate of arrival, declaration of intention, affidavits of witnesses, and petition for naturalization for Hedwig Maria Stauffer, also known as Hedy Lamarr, Hedwig Kiesler Mandl Markey, Hedy Kiesler, Hedy Mandl, and Hedy Markey." Ten digital objects (nine JPG pages of 0.56 to 1.80 MB, and a 5.6 MB PDF). I downloaded and read all nine pages [P]: Declaration of Intention (Form 2202-L-A, Los Angeles, 4 April 1939), Petition for Naturalization (Form N-405, no. 156727), oath of allegiance and witness affidavit (page 3), then three N-451 witness affidavit cards, each front and back (pages 4-9). No photograph appears. The declaration itself explains why: its footer reads "(DO NOT ATTACH PHOTOGRAPH TO THIS COPY OF DECLARATION)" and the text says "the photograph affixed to the duplicate and triplicate hereof is a likeness of the declarant"; the photographs went on other copies. The certificate itself (No. 7087294, noted on page 3 as issued 10 April 1953 in the name Hedy La Marr) is not in the file. The blog post's "Naturalization card" is an index card.
- Immigration A-file A 1 469 083 (visible on the petition): no NARA catalog record found [P].
- Patent: NAID 167820368, Patent Case File No. 2,292,387, Secret Communication System, inventors Hedy Kiesler Markey and George Antheil, "Unrestricted". 42 pages on Commons; I built a contact sheet of every page: forms, drawings, specification, signatures; no photograph [P].
