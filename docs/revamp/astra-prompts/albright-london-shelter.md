# Astra prompts: air-raid shelter in a London subway tunnel (as photographed 1940-1945)

Reference build: `assets-src/albright-london-shelter/`.

## Room prompt

Build a museum recreation of an air-raid shelter in a London subway tunnel with bunk beds, as in Toni
Frissell's photograph "[England during the war--subway tunnel with bunk beds]", between 1940 and 1945
(LoC 96506356, "No known restrictions on publication.", 827 x 1024). It is the room for Albright's
"Leaving home" chapter. One source says she hid in shelters as German bombs fell on London; no source
names the shelter: the label must say this is not one she is known to have used. No people. Black and
white: colours interpretive.

Label: "Recreation based on a photograph: an air-raid shelter in a London subway tunnel, with bunk beds,
Toni Frissell, between 1940 and 1945 (Library of Congress). One source says Madeleine Albright hid in
shelters as German bombs fell on London; no source names the shelter, and this is not one she is known
to have used. The people in the bunks are not shown. Colours are interpretive: the photograph is black
and white."

Layout: a rough-hewn, whitewashed arched tunnel about 2.7 m wide and 2.6 m high, 18 m long, bending
gently left at its far end, where a smooth-lined arched doorway opens onto a lit space. Along the right
wall, three-tier iron bunks (0.72 x 1.9 m, tiers at 0.38, 1.08, 1.78) every 2.05 m, canvas bottoms,
pale and dark blankets draped over the aisle edge, canvas awnings over the top tier hanging over the
aisle rail. A single bulb hangs from the crown near the camera. Path floor. Camera at (-0.65, -0.6,
1.45), looking 17 deg right of the axis, about 26 mm.

Lighting: very dark and contrasty, the bulb and the far doorway the only sources. Bake; bunks as
per-corner colour (COLOR_0). Export `albright-london-shelter.glb` / `-mobile`, hotspot_1..4,
artifact_tunnel, artifact_bunks, artifact_lamp, artifact_doorway, label_recreation, bounds.json.
