# Astra prompts: the high school building, Almonte, Ontario (about 1910)

Reference build: `assets-src/naismith-almonte-high-school/`.

## Room prompt

Build the street front of the Almonte high school building as a halftone print of about 1910 shows
it (McCord MP-0000.648.11: MODELLING REFERENCE ONLY, no pixels). Tour chapter: Naismith's schooling
in Almonte (single-source: began high school there in 1875). No source dates the building: the
label says it may not be the school he attended.

Layout (x right, y into the lot, front y = 0): a two-storey coursed-limestone building 23.4 x 16 m on
a 1.4 m raised basement, eave 10.7; quoined corners and a quoined central bay 5.1 m wide with paired
round-headed windows above an arched, gabled door hood; a stone stair enclosure with sloping sides
before the door; four 1.05 m windows per storey with dark sills and keystones; arched basement
windows; a hipped sheet-metal roof with a small central pediment, two chimneys and a belfry with
round-headed openings and a small dome. An elm and a picket fence at the left, trees at the right,
a gravel road and a plank sidewalk. Camera about (0, -36, 1.6), 37.2 mm, rise 0.18.

Label: "Recreation based on a photograph: the high school, Almonte, Ontario, about 1910 (halftone
print, McCord Stewart Museum MP-0000.648.11, used as a modelling reference). No source on file dates
the building, so it may not be the school James Naismith attended from 1875. People are not shown;
the sides and back of the building are not photographed."
