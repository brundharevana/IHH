# Astra prompts: the basketball tower, Naismith Memorial Basketball Hall of Fame (2019)

Reference build: `assets-src/naismith-hall-of-fame/`.

## Room prompt

Build the lawn by the tower that hoists a symbolic basketball outside the present Hall of Fame,
Springfield, Massachusetts, as Carol M. Highsmith's 2019 photographs show it (LoC 2019690414,
2019690512, public domain; the skyline may be used as a backdrop card). Tour chapter: why the Hall
honours him (enshrined 1959, before this building).

Layout: a white panelled tower 26 m, chamfered square shaft tapering 2.8 -> 1.4 m, an orange
basketball 3.4 m across in a cage of one horizontal and two vertical rings; a mown lawn, a red brick
path, a road with a curb and white lines, a retaining wall and the highway beyond, a row of young
trees, tall street lights; the downtown skyline as a card. Vehicles not modelled.

Label: "Recreation based on photographs: the tower hoisting a symbolic basketball outside the
present Naismith Memorial Basketball Hall of Fame, Springfield, Massachusetts, Carol M. Highsmith,
2019, Library of Congress (public domain). James Naismith was enshrined in 1959, before this
building; the photographs show the grounds today. Vehicles are not shown."
