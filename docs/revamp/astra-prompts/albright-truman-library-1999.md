# Astra prompts: interview set at the Truman Library (March 1999)

Reference build: `assets-src/albright-truman-library-1999/`.

## Room prompt

Build a museum recreation of the television interview set at the Truman Library, Independence,
Missouri, as photographed in March 1999 when Albright was there for the NATO enlargement ceremony
(Department of State photographs 59-PA-366-1, -2, -3, NARA, "Unrestricted"). The ceremony room is not
in these photographs and is not modelled; the room around the set is plain and dark. No people.

Label: "Recreation based on photographs: the interview set at the Truman Library, Independence,
Missouri, March 1999 (Department of State, National Archives), where Madeleine Albright gave interviews
when she was there for the NATO enlargement ceremony. Only the set is in the photographs: the drapes,
the flags, the chairs, the table and the lamp; the room around it is plain."

Layout: a wall of deep-pleated dark blue drapes; in front of it a tight row of national flags on gold
poles, hanging in folds, 0.30 m apart, in the order of frame -2: Greece, Iceland, Italy, Luxembourg,
Netherlands, Norway, Portugal, United Kingdom, Spain, Turkey, Hungary, United States, Poland; two carved
wooden armchairs (cabriole legs, pierced splat, cream seats) facing each other 1.55 m apart; a low
wooden table between them with a water glass; a black stage lamp on a tripod behind; a polished wooden
floor. Camera at (0, 0, 1.0), about 42 mm, looking at the flags.

Export `albright-truman-library-1999.glb` / `-mobile`, hotspot_1..4, artifact_* (drapes, chairs,
stage_lamp), label_recreation, bounds.json.
