## Room prompt

Build the Graben, Vienna, as the H. C. White Co. stereograph "The busy Graben" (copyright 1902, LoC
2019632509, public domain) shows it from an upper window: a street about 29 m between fronts, with
six-storey historicist street fronts on both sides (shops with awnings, regular upper windows,
string courses, cornices), an advertising column, a fountain with a statue in a round basin, a
gabled pavilion, the Plague Column further down, candelabra street lamps, granite setts and
sidewalks; the far end closed by buildings. Facade ornament is not readable at about 415 px per
view: generate facades after the window rhythm. People, cabs and carriages not modelled. Tour
chapter: Hedy Lamarr's birth in Vienna (1914); no source places her here.

Street fronts keep their tiled facade texture with baked light per corner (glTF COLOR_0).
