# Astra prompts: North Hall, University of Wisconsin (as photographed about 1885)

Reference build: `assets-src/muir-north-hall/`.

## Room prompt

Build an outdoor museum "room": the lawn before North Hall, University of Wisconsin, Madison, as in the
photograph UW-Madison Archives S05904, "North Hall", ca. 1885 ("No copyright (U.S.)"). One source says
John Muir lived in North Hall while a student (enrolled 1861, left 1863); the photograph is about 22
years later: say so in the label. Sepia print: colours interpretive. No interior.

Label: "Recreation based on a photograph: North Hall, University of Wisconsin, Madison, about 1885
(UW-Madison Archives). One source says John Muir lived in North Hall while a student (he enrolled in
1861 and left in 1863); the photograph was taken about 22 years later. Colours are interpretive: the
photograph is a sepia print."

Layout (from the photograph's two-point perspective; camera at the origin, 1.6 m, looking +y, 45 mm,
verticals parallel with lens rise 0.235): a plain four-storey block of coursed buff sandstone over a
basement, 28.2 x 12.8 m, cornice 15 m above the base course, low hipped roof with three chimneys;
corner at (-6.2, 47.6); long face 46.8 deg right of the view, short face 43.2 deg left. Long face: nine
window bays (two doors on the main floor), short face: two bays; windows 1.0 x 2.0 (top storey 1.6)
with white sashes, stone sills and lintels; small basement lights. Bare trees in front (tall trunks, fine
crowns, no leaves), three conifers at the sides, a boardwalk along the front, a rail fence at the left,
the lawn rising gently to the building. The two faces not photographed: plain stone.

Bake building and ground atlases with soft overcast light; trees and fence as per-corner colour
(COLOR_0). Export `muir-north-hall.glb` / `-mobile`, hotspot_1..4, artifact_north_hall,
artifact_bare_trees, label_recreation, bounds.json.
