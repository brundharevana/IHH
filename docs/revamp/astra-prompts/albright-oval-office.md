# Astra prompts: the Oval Office at Madeleine Albright's swearing-in (23 January 1997)

Reference build: `assets-src/albright-oval-office/`.

## Room prompt

Build a museum recreation of the Oval Office as photographed at Albright's swearing-in as Secretary of
State on 23 January 1997 (White House Photograph Office, Ralph Alswang, P49689-09a, NARA 131493882,
"Unrestricted"), with the window bay's form checked against Carol M. Highsmith's view of the office in
the 1980s (LoC 2011636529; its furnishings not modelled). Only the window bay, the flags, the desk and
the lectern are in the photographs: everything else in the oval is plain. No people.

Label: "Recreation based on photographs: the Oval Office as photographed at Madeleine Albright's
swearing-in as Secretary of State, 23 January 1997 (White House Photograph Office, Clinton Library /
National Archives), with the window bay's form checked against a 1980s view (Carol M. Highsmith,
Library of Congress). Only the window bay, the flags, the desk and the lectern are in the photographs;
the rest of the room is plain."

Layout: an oval 8.8 x 10.9 m, 5.6 m high, cream walls; in the curved wall behind the desk three tall
sash windows (1.2 wide, sill 0.7, head 4.1, six panes a sash) with full-height gold drapes and
valances beside each; the U.S. flag and the presidential flag on gold poles flanking the centre window,
hanging furled; a dark oak desk before the windows; a red-brown lectern with two black microphones and
a blue folder in front of it; plain dark blue-green carpet. Camera at (0.45, -1.8, 1.55) looking at the
centre window, about 40 mm.
