Albert Einstein

AI voice reading Einstein's own words, from his letter to President Franklin D. Roosevelt, 2 August 1939. The voice is synthetic, not Einstein's, and the mouth movement is computer-generated: Einstein was not filmed saying this.

How the mouth is driven

The ONNX export of Kokoro returns audio only, no phoneme durations. The timeline is therefore approximate: each caption phrase's Kokoro phonemes are spread evenly over that phrase's measured duration, mapped to an openness value (open vowels high, p/b/m closed), then gated by the audio loudness so silence closes the mouth.
