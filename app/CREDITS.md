Everything else in the landing scenes is made in code, with no downloaded models, HDRIs or textures:
the harbour (sky and water shaders, extruded steamship hull, pier, skyline), the bronze doors and stone
facade, the hall (niches, pilasters, coffers, reflective floor), the threads and atmosphere shaders, and
the stone, slab and bronze surface textures (drawn on a canvas at runtime, src/landing/scenes/materials.js).
Lighting uses drei `<Environment>` with `<Lightformer>` children (rendered locally, no HDRI fetched).

Inductee story points: verbatim `claim` text of facts with status `verified` in
data/inductees/<slug>/facts.jsonl, each with its first evidence source linked. Built by
src/landing/data/build_inductees.py into src/landing/data/inductees.json.

Mission wording: "Immigrants built America. We're building a museum." quoted from the Immigrant Hall of
Honor home page, https://immigranthallofhonor.org/ (fetched 2026-10-14; shown in sentence case, the site
sets it in capitals).
